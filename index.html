About

A small, independent software company

Ledger Innovation is an independent software company. We build products of our own, publish most of the code, and run the infrastructure behind them ourselves.

Our longest-running product, Ecency, has served a global community of writers and readers for years, on web and on both mobile platforms. Around it grew a family of tools for identity, search, wallets and payments.
