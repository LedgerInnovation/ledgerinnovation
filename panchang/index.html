Panchang

Choghadiya & Hora, live and fully offline

The live Hindu almanac for wherever you are, computed entirely on your phone, fully offline, with no account and no tracking.

App Store Google Play Privacy Policy
Features

Everything the almanac tracks, on your phone

Choghadiya, Hora, Kaal, Muhurta and Ekadashi, computed for your city and your day.

Choghadiya & Hora

All 16 choghadiya and 24 planetary hours with live countdowns, plus each hora's beej mantra.

Kaal & Muhurta

Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika Kaal and the auspicious Abhijit Muhurta, with on-device alerts.

Full Panchang

Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Paksha, Masa, Ritu and Moon Rashi (Lahiri ayanamsa).

Ekadashi

Fasting days with traditional names - Nirjala, Devshayani and more - plus sunrise reminders.

Works anywhere

33,000+ cities with offline search, 31 languages, dark mode and smart quiet hours.

Private by design

No servers, no account, no analytics. The app collects no data at all.
