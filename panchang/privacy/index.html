Panchang

Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Panchang is a mobile app for Choghadiya, Hora, Rahu Kaal, Ekadashi and the rest of the Hindu Panchang. It is published by Ledger Innovation MB, Lithuania, European Union (“we”). The app is built to be private by design: it has no servers and collects nothing itself. The only data that leaves your phone is for an optional ad you choose to watch.

What the app does with information

  • Location. If you tap “Use my location”, the app reads your device position once to find your nearest city and compute local sunrise/sunset times. This happens entirely on your device. Your location is never transmitted anywhere, since the app has no backend to send it to. You can also skip location entirely and pick a city from the bundled offline list.
  • Birth details (Personal Panchang). If you use the optional Personal Panchang feature, the birth date, time and place you enter are stored only in the app’s local storage on your device and used only for on-device calculations. They are never uploaded, shared or backed up by us.
  • Settings. Your preferences (chosen city, alert toggles, language, display options) are stored locally on your device.
  • Notifications. All alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification service is used; no push tokens exist.

Optional rewarded ads

You can unlock Personal Panchang for one day by watching a rewarded video ad. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and are loaded only when you tap “Watch an ad”. If you never tap it, the ad SDK sends nothing.

When you do, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address, approximate location derived from it and ad interaction data, to serve and measure the ad and to prevent fraud. Before the first ad you are asked for consent where the law requires it (Google’s consent form in the EEA, UK and some US states; on iOS, Apple’s tracking prompt). You can decline, and you will then see limited or non-personalized ads, or none. Your birth details, chosen city and settings are never shared with the ad provider. See how Google uses information from ads.

The lifetime purchase shows no ads.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or profiles.
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs.
  • No servers of our own; we receive no data.
  • Your birth details, location and settings are never transmitted.

Purchases

The one-time “Personal Panchang” unlock is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment information. The unlock status is saved locally on your device and you can restore it through the store’s standard “Restore purchases” mechanism.

Data sources

City names and coordinates are from the GeoNames gazetteer (geonames.org, CC-BY 4.0), bundled inside the app and queried offline.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It collects no data itself; ads are requested only when a user chooses to watch one.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if a future version adds an online feature, the updated policy will be posted at this address before the change ships.
